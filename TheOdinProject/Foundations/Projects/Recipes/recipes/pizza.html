Paneer Butter Masala Pizza Recipe

Description

Instead of standard pizza sauce, this recipe uses a thick paneer makhani reduction spread over a crispy pizza base. It is topped with marinated paneer cubes, crunchy onions, and a thick blanket of melted mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

  • Base: 1 large pre-baked pizza crust or stretched fresh pizza dough
  • Pizza Sauce:
    • ½ cup thick paneer butter masala gravy (reduced until thick so the crust doesn't get soggy)
  • Toppings:
    • 1 cup paneer cubes (small)
    • ½ red onion (sliced)
    • ½ capsicum (sliced)
    • 1 green chili (finely chopped, optional)
  • Cheese:
    • 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Garnish:
    • A sprinkle of kasuri methi
    • ½ tsp chaat masala
    • Fresh cilantro leaves

Steps

  1. Preheat the Oven:
    • Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.
  2. Prepare the Sauce Layer:
    • Place your pizza crust or rolled dough onto a baking sheet or pizza peel.
    • Spread the thickened paneer butter masala gravy evenly across the surface, leaving a small border around the edges for the crust.
  3. Assemble the Toppings:
    • Scatter half of the shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce layer.
    • Evenly distribute the paneer cubes, sliced red onions, capsicum, and green chilies across the pizza.
    • Cover the toppings with the remaining mozzarella cheese.
  4. Bake:
    • Transfer the pizza to the oven and bake for 12–15 minutes, or until the crust turns golden-brown and the cheese is bubbling with brown spots.
  5. Garnish and Serve:
    • Remove from the oven and immediately dust with a pinch of crushed kasuri methi and chaat masala.
    • Top with fresh cilantro leaves, slice, and serve hot.
