Creamy Makhani Sauce Pasta Recipe

Description

This comforting dish replaces traditional Italian tomato marinara with a smooth, luxurious North Indian Makhani (butter masala) gravy. The vibrant orange sauce clings beautifully to penne or macaroni, giving you a restaurant-style experience at home.

Ingredients

  • Pasta: 2 cups Penne, Fusilli, or Macaroni (boiled al dente)
  • Aromatics:
    • 1 tbsp butter
    • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
    • 1 small onion (pureed)
  • Makhani Base:
    • 3 large tomatoes (pureed)
    • 10-12 soaked cashews (blended into a smooth paste)
  • Spices:
    • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
    • ½ tsp garam masala
    • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
    • Salt to taste
  • Finishers:
    • 3 tbsp fresh heavy cream
    • Fresh coriander leaves (chopped)
    • Grated processed or parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the Pasta:
    • Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Prepare the Makhani Base:
    • Melt butter in a pan. Add the pureed onion and ginger-garlic paste. Sauté until the raw smell disappears.
    • Pour in the tomato purée and cook for 5–6 minutes until the oil starts separating from the sides.
    • Stir in the smooth cashew paste and mix thoroughly.
  3. Season the Sauce:
    • Add the Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Simmer on low heat for 2–3 minutes.
    • Crush the kasuri methi between your palms and stir it into the gravy.
  4. Combine and Finish:
    • Lower the flame and pour in the fresh heavy cream, stirring continuously to create a smooth, velvety orange sauce.
    • Toss the boiled pasta into the simmering makhani sauce until every piece is evenly coated.
    • Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and a generous sprinkle of grated cheese before serving hot.
