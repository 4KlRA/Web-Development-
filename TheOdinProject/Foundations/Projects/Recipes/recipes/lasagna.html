Indian-style vegetable and paneer lasagna Recipe

Description

An Indian-style vegetable and paneer lasagna blends classic Italian pasta-layering with vibrant Indian spices, a spiced tomato-masala sauce, creamy white sauce, and crumbled paneer.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna Noodles: 9 no-boil (or pre-cooked) lasagna sheets
  • Spiced Tomato Sauce:
    • 2.5 cups marinara or tomato purée
    • ½ tsp red chili powder or Kashmiri chili powder
    • ½ tsp garam masala
    • A pinch of cardamom powder
    • ½ tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
    • 1 tbsp butter or heavy cream
  • Vegetable & Paneer Filling:
    • 1 tbsp oil
    • 1 tsp minced garlic and ginger
    • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
    • Mixed vegetables (carrot, bell peppers, baby spinach, corn)
    • ½ tsp cumin and garam masala
    • 2 cups grated or crumbled paneer
  • White Sauce (Béchamel) & Cheese:
    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
    • 1.5 to 2 cups milk
    • Salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning
    • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Make the Spiced Tomato Sauce:
    • Warm butter in a pan, add the marinara/tomato purée, red chili powder, garam masala, cardamom powder, and kasuri methi.
    • Simmer for 5–7 minutes until thick, then stir in a splash of heavy cream if desired.
  2. Sauté the Veggies & Paneer:
    • Heat oil in a separate pan; sauté ginger, garlic, and onions until translucent.
    • Add the chopped mixed vegetables, cumin, and garam masala; cook until tender-crisp.
    • Fold in the crumbled paneer and baby spinach until the spinach wilts, then set aside.
  3. Prepare the White Sauce:
    • Melt butter in a saucepan, whisk in the flour for a minute, then slowly pour in milk while whisking constantly to avoid lumps.
    • Season with salt, pepper, and Italian herbs until smooth and slightly thickened.
  4. Assemble the Lasagna:
    • Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce at the bottom of a greased baking dish.
    • Place a layer of lasagna noodles.
    • Add a layer of the paneer-vegetable mixture, a drizzle of white sauce, and a spoonful of tomato sauce.
    • Repeat the layers (noodles -> veggies/paneer -> sauces -> mozzarella) for 3 to 4 tiers, finishing with a top layer of white sauce and a generous blanket of mozzarella cheese.
  5. Bake:
    • Cover the dish with greased aluminum foil and bake at 425°F (220°C) for 25–30 minutes.
    • Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 10–12 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden-brown.
    • Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
